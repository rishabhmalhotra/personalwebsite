The architecture behind ultra-low-latency, end-to-end live streaming auctions

Ultra-Low Latency

End-to-end streaming architecture engineered to shave milliseconds off every hop — from ingest through transcoding, distribution, and playback — so bids and offers land in near real-time.

Trust Layer

The trust component that makes an auction on a live stream possible: verification, integrity checks, and reliability guarantees that keep every bid binding even at auction velocity.

Auction on a Stream

Real-time bidding fused with live video — where the clock, the feed, and the ledger all have to agree. This is the hard, interesting part.

Trust at auction speed. Building the reliability and integrity systems that make a live-streamed auction trustworthy, auditable, and resilient under load.
